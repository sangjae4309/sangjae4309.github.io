## research

***

My research interests are in computer architecture and hardware design, with a focus on **AI-based FPGA accelerators**, **HLS physical co-optimization**, and **memory systems**.

I am especially interested in building efficient accelerator systems for modern AI workloads and improving the hardware design flow across high-level synthesis, physical implementation, and architecture-level exploration. My previous work studied memory reliability and protection mechanisms for resilient memory systems.
